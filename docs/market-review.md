# TurfGo market review (November 2026)

I wrote this before deciding whether to launch TurfGo in Madurai, Tamil Nadu. It compares TurfGo, as built at the time, with the apps and habits it would have competed against. Facts about competitors come from public sources, listed at the end, as seen on 2026-11-20.

## Verdict

TurfGo had no users, no store listing and no signed turfs. It would have launched in a city where Playo already listed 62 bookable venues. Its real advantages were a small upfront payment (a 20% advance) and no player convenience fee. Neither mattered without turfs and players. The deciding factor in this market is supply, meaning turfs signed up face to face, not software features. So I stopped.

## Who TurfGo would have competed with

| Competitor | Presence in Madurai | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Playo** | 62 venues listed (45 football), all bookable | National leader. 4.7★ from 16k App Store ratings. Pays venues weekly. Has game-finding and social features. |
| **Playspots** | 9 venues | Smaller aggregator based in Kerala |
| **Turf Town** | City page exists, no venues named | Chennai-based. Strong social and tournament features. |
| **Hudle** | 1 pickleball venue | 0% commission for venues, full venue-management software, raised $2.5M in 2025 |
| **Phone / WhatsApp / Instagram** | Every turf | Free, instant, cash to the owner. Many owners' real "competitor". |
| **OneOS, Thidal** | Selling to Madurai owners | Booking software for owners, OneOS at 0% commission |

Even with every planned feature shipped, TurfGo would have scored below Playo, Hudle and plain phone booking.

## Why players and owners would have chosen something else

1. **No turfs.** A marketplace with nothing to book has nothing to offer.
2. **The commission was wrong for owners.** TurfGo took 13% of each booking. Hudle and OneOS charge venues 0%.
3. **Owners weren't notified of bookings.** There were no push or WhatsApp alerts, while a phone call reaches them instantly.
4. **There was no payout system.** Owners would have had no view of what they were owed.
5. **There was no social layer.** People play football and cricket in groups, and Playo, Turf Town and Hudle all help players find games and teammates.

## What TurfGo did better

- **A smaller upfront payment than Playo:** a 20% advance, where Playo's partner docs say the player pays the full amount online.
- **No player convenience fee,** where Playo and Hudle both charge a non-refundable one.
- **A clear refund rule in code:** cancelling more than 24 hours ahead gives a full refund of the advance, automatically.
- **A local founder,** who could have signed turfs in person. Most of Playo's Madurai listings have only one or two reviews.

## What I'd do differently

Validate first: talk to ten turf owners about what they pay, what annoys them and what they'd switch for, before writing any code. Then set prices against what they already use. A commission rate picked before talking to owners turned out to be the weakest part of the plan.
